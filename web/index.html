    General notes

    1. Each sheet is one chore from the project's 20-chore eval set. Each revision is one real simulate call, and the report under it is word for word what the model would read back.
    2. A part joins the chain when a chain part touches it and the push changes where it goes. The simulator runs every machine twice in lockstep, with and without the first push. A part counts once it's at least 5 cm off its push-free path and has itself moved at least 5 cm.
    3. A machine works only when a chain of at least 5 parts reaches the finale, and nothing reaches the finale when nobody pushes.
    4. Your browser re-runs every revision you open from its blueprint and checks the run's trace hash against the one recorded in Node. The balloons number the chain in order; the red pencil marks where each link happened.
